# Family Quick Start Guide

## Welcome

Welcome to the WoogleNet family services platform.

Your account gives you access to services that may include:

- Family File Storage
- Shared Documents
- Personal Documents
- Music Streaming
- Audiobooks
- Media Libraries
- Family Knowledge Resources

Most services are available from your web browser and approved devices.

---

# Your Account

You will receive:

- Username
- Temporary Password
- List of Services You Can Access

At first login:

1. Sign in
2. Change your password
3. Verify you can access your assigned services

If you have trouble signing in, contact the system administrator.

---

# Password Guidelines

Use a password that is:

✅ Unique

✅ Easy for you to remember

✅ Difficult for others to guess

Avoid:

❌ Birthdays

❌ Pet names

❌ Common words

❌ Reusing passwords from other websites

---

# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

For most family users:

MFA is recommended but optional.

If you choose to enable MFA:

1. Install an authenticator app
2. Scan the QR code
3. Save your recovery code

Common authenticator apps:

- Microsoft Authenticator
- Google Authenticator
- Authy
- Bitwarden Authenticator

Setup usually takes less than 5 minutes.

---

# Streaming Devices

Examples:

- Roku
- Apple TV
- Fire TV
- Google TV
- Smart TVs

No MFA is required for streaming devices.

Simply sign in with the account provided for media access.

Do not use administrator accounts on streaming devices.

# Security Tips

Always:

✅ Sign out from shared devices

✅ Keep passwords private

✅ Report unusual activity

✅ Use your own account

Never:

❌ Share passwords

❌ Share recovery codes

❌ Use administrator accounts on media devices

❌ Store passwords in plain text
